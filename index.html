How to play

Help

Answer each Kahoot-style question before the timer runs out.

A correct answer moves your ship forward. One wrong answer moves your ship backwards.

Get three correct answers in a row to ready a bonus. Your next correct answer moves two spaces, then the bonus goes back to normal.

The first racer to move forward 10 spaces wins. Questions are randomly picked from the 15-question bank.

The opponent ships can answer right or wrong too. Hard difficulty makes them faster and more accurate.

In Flappy Fleet, tap, click, or press Space to keep your ship flying through the harbour. The level speeds up as your score grows.

Every checkpoint pauses Flappy Fleet for three random quiz questions. After each quiz section, the harbour gets harder and the pipes move closer together.
